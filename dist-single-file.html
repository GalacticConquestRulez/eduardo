About Dog Grooming USA

A grooming-first philosophy, since 2017.

Dog Grooming USA began serving South Florida in 2017 with a grooming-first philosophy centered around quality, trust, and dependable pet care.

Today the company continues to grow while expanding its grooming, daycare, hotel, and educational offerings.

Our goal is simple: provide professional pet care that families feel confident returning to again and again.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Dog Grooming USA (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you submit an appointment request, we collect your name, email address, phone number, the service you are interested in, and anything you choose to tell us about your dog.
  • Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our host may record standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, and the pages you visit.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your appointment request and schedule your visit.
  • To contact you about your pet's care, appointment reminders, or follow-up questions.
  • To answer questions about our services, the Groomer Artist Academy, or franchise inquiries.
  • To improve our website and the services we offer.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate this website and manage appointments, and only to the extent needed to do that work — or where we are required to by law.

Text messages and calls

If you provide a phone number, you agree that we may contact you at that number about your appointment. Message and data rates may apply. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time.

Cookies

This site uses only the cookies required for basic operation and, where enabled, standard analytics. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings; the site will continue to work without them.

Data retention and security

We keep appointment information for as long as needed to serve you and to meet our record-keeping obligations. We use reasonable safeguards to protect it, though no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Children's privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the information you have given us. Contact us using the details below and we will respond promptly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
