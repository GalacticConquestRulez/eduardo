# Image slots — what to drop in, and where

Every picture on the page is a plain `<img>` pointing at a file in `assets/img/`.
**Swapping in a real photograph is a one-line `src` change per slot.** Keep the
listed aspect ratio and the layout will not move.

## Why the artwork is vector right now

The stock-photo CDNs (Pexels, Unsplash, Pixabay, Wikimedia) are blocked by the
egress policy of the environment this page was built in, so no photograph could
be downloaded or even verified from here. Rather than hardcode stock URLs that
might 404 in production, the page ships with **original SVG artwork we own
outright** — no licence, no attribution, no expiry — generated by
`tools/gen_art.py`. It is a real art-directed system, not grey boxes, so the
page is presentable as-is and improves the moment real photography lands.

## Slots

| Slot | File | Ratio | What the photo should show |
|---|---|---|---|

| Service — grooming | `svc-grooming.svg` | 4:3 | Shears or clipper work on a finished coat, close in. |
| Service — daycare | `svc-daycare.svg` | 4:3 | Dogs playing under supervision in the indoor space. |
| Service — hotel | `svc-hotel.svg` | 4:3 | A settled dog in overnight accommodation. |
| Before ×3 | `ba-{poodle,pomeranian,yorkie}-before.svg` | 1:1 | **Same dog, same angle, same framing as the matching after.** The comparison slider only reads correctly if the camera did not move. |

## Swapping in photographs

1. Export each photo at roughly **2× its display size**, cropped to the ratio above.
2. Save as `.jpg` (photos) or `.webp` (smaller, well supported) into `assets/img/`.
3. Change the `src` — and the extension in the filename — in `index.html`.
4. Update the `alt` text to describe that specific photo. Alt text is how the
   page reads to screen readers and to Google.
5. Leave the `width`/`height` attributes set to the real pixel dimensions. They
   reserve the space and stop the page jumping as images load.

Do not remove `loading="lazy"` from below-the-fold images, and keep
`fetchpriority="high"` on the hero.

### Sourcing royalty-free photos

Pexels and Unsplash both licence free for commercial use with no attribution
required. Useful searches: *dog grooming*, *pet groomer*, *dog bath*,
*dog salon*, *groomer scissors*, *dog daycare*. Download the largest size
offered, then crop to the ratios above.

Real photography of your own shop will always outperform stock here —
particularly for the before/after sliders and the facility tiles, which are
specifically about *your* space. Stock is the fallback, not the goal.

## The logo

`assets/img/logo.svg` is a placeholder lockup — a geometric dog-head mark, a
`DOG GROOMING` wordmark, and a spaced `U S A` line in the brand red. It is
referenced in exactly three places:

- `index.html` header (`.brand img`)
- `privacy.html` header
- `assets/img/logo-mark-light.svg` in the footer

When the improved logo arrives, overwrite these four files, keeping the same
filenames and roughly the same proportions:

| File | Size | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `logo.svg` | ~300×64 | Full lockup, header |
| `logo-mark.svg` | 64×64 | Square mark, dark background |
| `logo-mark-light.svg` | 64×64 | Square mark, light-on-dark (footer) |
| `favicon.svg` | 32×32 | Browser tab |

Nothing else needs to change.

## Regenerating the artwork

```bash
python3 tools/gen_art.py assets/img
```

Brand colours, the icon set, and the plate composition all live at the top of
that file. Changing `SCHEMES` there restyles every plate at once.
