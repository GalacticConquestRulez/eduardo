The offer

What we build for you.

Every piece of this is something you just watched. Nothing on this list is theoretical — it's the same cameras, the same edit, the same grade, pointed at your brand.

Aerials Drone
Establishing shots, reveals, orbits and top-downs that place your location in its world. Golden hour is scheduled, not hoped for.
Ground cinema Ronin 4D
Full-frame cinema on a four-axis gimbal: walk-throughs, product moves and portraits that float. Interiors, people, and detail — the part aerials can't do.
Stills Gallery-grade
A dedicated photo pass on shoot day, graded to match the film, delivered as a gallery like the one above — web-ready and print-ready.
Delivery Masters + cuts
4K masters. One 2.39:1 hero cut, a 16:9 web cut, and 9:16 vertical cuts for social. Color graded, licensed music, captions on the social versions.
Landing page Web
A single page, like this one, built to hold the film and convert the visitor. Fast, mobile-first, yours to keep.
Boutique & merch Optional
Product and merchandising direction so the film, the stills, the page and the shelf all speak with one voice.
The plan

From Monday to launch.

Five steps, in order. The first one is already on your calendar.

  1. Follow-up call Mon Sep 7 · 2:00 PM

    Lock scope, locations and dates. You bring the must-have shots; Max brings the shot list from what you've watched here.

  2. Shot plan Within 48 hours of the call

    A written shot list, schedule, and music direction for your sign-off. Nothing gets filmed that you haven't seen on paper.

  3. Shoot days Drone + Ronin 4D

    Aerials and ground cinema on the same days, so the light and the look match. Stills pass included.

  4. Edit & grade First cut, then two revision rounds

    You review a private link, mark timestamps, and we cut again. Color, sound and titles are finished before the final master is rendered.

  5. Launch Masters, cuts, gallery, page

    Everything delivered in one folder, the landing page live, social cuts ready to schedule.
