Scope of work

Growth & investor confidence plan.

Green Flash Advertising's execution plan for Dog Grooming USA and Groomer Artist Academy across three fronts: client acquisition at each location, investor-facing brand assets, and a managed presence across every major advertising and social platform. It also details the operational systems Green Flash will deploy and maintain.

Pillar 1 · Client acquisition

A funnel for every door.

Each location receives its own dedicated acquisition funnel, managed and optimized by Green Flash on a continuous basis. Groomer Artist Academy receives a fully independent funnel, built and managed to one objective: enrolling new artists.

Location funnel

  • Geo-targeted ad campaigns per location, refined against booking data as it accumulates
  • High-definition landing page per location, built as a single-action conversion portal separate from the main site
  • Per-location local SEO so organic and paid traffic both resolve to the correct store page
  • Retargeting sequences for visitors who did not book

Academy funnel

  • Campaigns placed within grooming and trade-content audiences rather than generic education targeting
  • The training film as the centerpiece creative across every Academy campaign
  • Dedicated enrollment landing page with a single conversion path
  • AI scheduling agent handling enrollment inquiries and booking consultations in real time
  • Graduate work and student results captured and repurposed as recruiting creative for subsequent cohorts
Pillar 2 · Investor-facing assets

Assets that carry the investor conversation.

Green Flash and Drone God Max will produce and maintain the brand assets that carry investor conversations.

  • Investor film — drone cinematography, Ronin 6K, custom graphics; produced by Drone God Max
  • Corporate website presenting the full footprint, the vet-group relationship, and both revenue streams in one place
  • One-tap language switch on both websites and every landing page — English, Spanish, Haitian Creole, and Portuguese
  • Consistent daily publishing across all accounts, demonstrating active momentum whenever an investor looks
  • Unified brand execution across site, social, and video
Pillar 3 · Social media & platform presence

Every platform, with a job.

Green Flash will establish and manage a presence across every major platform, each assigned a defined role in the funnel.

PlatformRole in the strategy
Facebook & InstagramPrimary local booking and enrollment campaigns (Meta), plus daily organic posts and stories per account
TikTokShort-form technique clips, transformations, and behind-the-scenes content built for reach and audience growth
GoogleSearch campaigns capturing active local intent, plus Google Business Profile management per location

Account structure

Fully separate social profiles and separate Meta ad accounts for Groomer Artist Academy and for the grooming locations — independent creative, independent budgets, and clean reporting on each.

Content production

Green Flash will script, film, edit, and schedule all content, testing formats for each location and the Academy, and scaling what performs.

  • Comedic content built for shares and organic reach
  • Scripted content centered on the brand's grooming and hair-care techniques
  • Client testimonials and before-and-after showcases
  • User-generated-style video native to each platform
  • Drone and cinematic visuals from Drone God Max for every location and landing page

Proprietary content engine

Green Flash operates proprietary in-house software that ingests client content, edits it, and schedules posting across every account. The daily posting commitment is systematized rather than dependent on manual effort.

  • Ingests raw footage from shoots and staff uploads, edits it, and schedules posts across all accounts
  • Analyzes top-performing content in the category and generates comparable concepts
  • Surfaces content ideas and assists with scripting for every location and the Academy
  • Tracks organic performance so ad spend is placed behind content that has already proven itself

Paid promotion is applied only to posts with demonstrated organic traction, so budget amplifies what is working instead of testing from zero.

Production cadence

Biweekly shoots. Fly-Throughs. Wag-Throughs.

Drone God Max will shoot on site every two weeks at each location, and will train staff to capture and upload content to a shared Google Drive between shoots, so content is captured continuously.

Biweekly shoots & staff training

  • In-person shoot every two weeks per location
  • Hands-on training for employees on filming and uploading to Google Drive
  • Staff-captured footage folded into the editing and scheduling pipeline

Fly-Throughs & Wag-Throughs

Every location receives two signature pieces: a drone Fly-Through and a Wag-Through — a camera mounted on Cashews for a first-person run through the space.

  • Wag-Through and Fly-Through of the new flagship location
  • Wag-Through and Fly-Through of the existing Miami Lakes location
  • Both produced as standard for every future location
Operational systems

From ad to paid booking in one path.

The systems Green Flash will configure, connect, and run so a customer moves from ad to landing page to paid booking without a gap, and no inquiry waits on an email.

Calendly — booking & payments

  • Per-location booking embedded on each store page and landing page
  • Facebook integration for booking directly from ads and the Facebook page
  • Payment collected at time of booking
  • Automated confirmations and reminders
  • Booking conversions reported back to ad platforms, so optimization is driven by completed bookings rather than clicks

AI scheduling agent

  • Immediate 24/7 response to booking and enrollment inquiries
  • Handles standard questions on services, pricing, and course details
  • Books directly into Calendly
  • Scales across additional locations with no added headcount

Ad platform management

All ad accounts set up, connected, and managed under a single point of control.

  • Google Ads
  • TikTok Ads
  • Reddit Ads
  • Snapchat Ads
